About Me

I am Ming Yang (杨铭), a first-year Ph.D. student in the M.S.-Ph.D. track at the School of Artificial Intelligence of Nanjing University, advised by Professor Yu-Feng Li (李宇峰), and a member of the LAMDA Group (机器学习与数据挖掘研究所), led by Professor Zhi-Hua Zhou (周志华).

Biography

2024.09 - Present M.S.-Ph.D. Program in Artificial Intelligence, Nanjing University (Ph.D. stage from 2026.09)
2020.09 - 2024.06 B.Sc. in Computer Science and Technology, Nanjing University of Aeronautics and Astronautics

Research Interests

My research focuses on reliable multimodal intelligence, particularly reasoning, planning, and test-time adaptation. I also explore neuro-symbolic methods and agentic foundation models for remote sensing.
